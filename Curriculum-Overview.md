## 8-Week Progressive Curriculum (Month 1 & Month 2)

### Month 1: Core Spoken Fluency, Meetings & Technical Accuracy

#### Week 1 (Lesson 01): Foundations of Tech Fluency & Accent Reset
- **Phonetics Focus**: Tech stress-timing (*de-VEL-op-ment*, *com-PO-nent*, *AU-to-mo-tive*), the aspirated vs. silent /h/, and past-tense `-ed` endings.
- **Lexical Agility**: The "Anti-Freeze" Discourse Toolkit & 3-way circumlocution formulas.
- **Workplace Simulation**: The 90-Second Sprint Standup & "Day in the Life" elevator pitch.

#### Week 2 (Lesson 02): Agile Standups, Sprint Reviews & Clarification Diplomacy
- **Phonetics Focus**: Consonant-to-vowel linking (catenation: *hold on*, *first of all*) and eliminating glottal stops.
- **Lexical Agility**: Phrasal verbs in tech (*roll out, drill down, spin up, rule out, wrap up, walk through*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Leading a daily standup, addressing blockers, and diplomatically asking foreign colleagues to clarify or slow down without feeling awkward.

#### Week 3 (Lesson 03): Client Communication, Scope Creep & Diplomatic Pushback
- **Phonetics Focus**: Pitch intonation for diplomatic politeness (avoiding flat French intonation that can sound unintentional blunt).
- **Lexical Agility**: Softening language and diplomatic hedges (*"To be fair...", "From an architectural standpoint...", "That might introduce some latency..."*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Handling an urgent request from an automotive client who wants a major ERP feature change right before production release.

#### Week 4 (Lesson 04): The Technical Demo & Module Walkthrough Lab
- **Phonetics Focus**: Chunking and pacing: delivering information in digestible rhythmic groups.
- **Lexical Agility**: Signposting language (*"Moving on to...", "If you look at the top-right pane...", "Under the hood, this triggers..."*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Delivering a 5-minute live screen-share walkthrough demo of the automotive spare parts catalog sync.

---

### Month 2: Advanced Technical Influence, Troubleshooting & Executive Presence

#### Week 5 (Lesson 05): International Teamwork & Natural Pre-Meeting Small Talk
- **Phonetics Focus**: Contractions and weak forms in casual conversation (*could've, would've, wanna, gonna* vs. formal).
- **Lexical Agility**: Effortless transitions from informal small talk (*weather, travel, food, weekend*) to formal agenda items.
- **Workplace Simulation**: Managing the first 5 minutes of a call with international colleagues before the main agenda kicks off.

#### Week 6 (Lesson 06): Incident Management, Bug Reports & Post-Mortem Debriefs
- **Phonetics Focus**: Emotional neutrality and vocal steadiness under pressure.
- **Lexical Agility**: High-precision problem/solution vocabulary (*glitch, anomaly, rollback, patch, redundancy, failover*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Leading an incident post-mortem after an ERP database sync failure in production.

#### Week 7 (Lesson 07): Automotive Supply Chain & ERP Logistics Terminology
- **Phonetics Focus**: Numbers, metric units, serial identifiers, and complex compound nouns.
- **Lexical Agility**: Domain-specific collocations (*inventory turnover, lead times, SKU mapping, warehouse dispatch, parts reconciliation*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Reviewing ERP supply-chain metrics with an overseas logistics director.

#### Week 8 (Lesson 08): Executive Presence & Spontaneous Technical Debate
- **Phonetics Focus**: Sentence stress for persuasion and contrastive emphasis (*"We don't need to rebuild it; we just need to optimize it"*).
- **Lexical Agility**: High-level argumentation formulas (*"Granted, X has advantages; however, Y mitigates our long-term risk"*).
- **Workplace Simulation**: Spontaneous debate on Cloud SaaS vs. On-Premises ERP deployment for automotive suppliers.
