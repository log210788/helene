## 🎯 Teaching Philosophy for This Lesson
In marketing, clients and internal stakeholders constantly ask for *"one last campaign tweak"* right before launch—extra ad variants, a last-minute landing page, or rushing an unvetted brief. French speakers often sound either **too blunt** when translating *"Non, ce n'est pas possible"* or drop aspirated `/h/` and `-ed` endings when defending a campaign timeline under pressure.
- **Your job:** Teach her the **3-beat Diplomatic Marketing Pushback Rhythm**: *(1) Validate the goal → (2) Show the budget/timeline/brand trade-off → (3) Propose Phase 2 or a smart alternative*.
- **Correction Rule:** Do **on-the-spot gentle taps** for `/h/` and `-ed` during the warm-up ONLY; during the live marketing role-play, save corrections for after the scene so she stays persuasive and natural.

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Script

### 1. Phonetics & Marketing Week Check-In (00:00 – 08:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **How to teach French `/h/`:** Tell Helene: *"Imagine fogging up a mirror right before the vowel: `hhh-ype`, `hhh-eadline`, `hhh-ighlights`, `hhh-andle`."* Contrast with silent `/h/` in `hour` and `honest`.
- **How to teach `-ed` endings in marketing verbs:** Don't lecture—just contrast out loud:
  - `launched` (`/t/`), `pitched` (`/t/`), `briefed` (`/t/`) — crisp, zero extra syllable
  - `targeted` (`/ɪd/`), `updated` (`/ɪd/`), `tested` (`/ɪd/`) — extra syllable because the base ends in `/t/` or `/d/`
  - `rolled out` — link `/d/` into `out`: *roll-dout*.
- **Quick Chat (3 mins):** *"How was your week in marketing? Any last-minute campaign changes, tight turnarounds, or demanding briefs?"* (Note 1–2 real campaign details she mentions and weave them into Step 3!)

### 2. Core Toolkit: 6 Marketing Pushback Moves (08:00 – 22:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **Worksheet Part 2 Answer Key:** `1. park that` • `2. trade-off` • `3. aligned on priorities` • `4. drill down into` • `5. ruled out` • `6. wrap up`.
- **How to teach each phrase:**
  1. Have Helene complete the 6 blanks on her worksheet (3 mins).
  2. Read each line aloud and have her **shadow your intonation**—especially the warm, collaborative rise on *"That's a strong angle—"* before the calm downward drop on *"let's park that for Wave 2."*
  3. **3-Second Rapid-Fire Stress Test:** Throw 3 unrealistic marketing demands at her and make her fire back in under 3 seconds using a toolkit phrase:
     - *"Can we shoot a brand-new video ad by tomorrow morning?"* → (Target: *ruled out* / *trade-off*)
     - *"Let's add three more audience segments to Friday's email blast."* → (Target: *aligned on priorities*)
     - *"The CEO wants to change the tagline right now."* → (Target: *drill down into* the core objective)

### 3. Live Role-Play: Last-Minute Campaign Scope Creep (22:00 – 43:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- **Setup (2 mins):** *"I'm an impatient Brand Director / Client 48 hours before your big Q4 campaign launch. Halfway through the creative review, I interrupt and demand a brand-new influencer reel AND a redesigned landing page before Monday's go-live. Your goal: protect campaign quality, budget, and launch date without losing my buy-in."*
- **Run 1 — Full Scene (6 mins):** Start enthusiastic, then push hard: *"Look, the visuals are great, but my boss just saw a competitor's TikTok—we need an influencer reel and a new landing page live by Monday morning. Surely the agency can whip that up?"*
  - Push back twice (*"Monday is non-negotiable for our Q4 KPIs..."*) so she has to deploy **trade-off**, **ruled out**, **aligned on priorities**, and **park that for Wave 2**.
- **Micro-Coaching (4 mins):** Give 2 praise points + 2 upgrades (e.g., upgrading *"We don't have time"* → *"The trade-off of adding a new landing page now is diluting our QA and risking Monday's launch."*).
- **Run 2 — Replay with Resolution (6 mins):** Re-run the last 90 seconds—this time you agree to Wave 2, and she must **wrap up** crisply with clear deliverables, owners, and KPIs.

### 4. Feedback & Homework (43:00 – 50:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 4.*
- Celebrate 3 fluency/persuasion wins + 2 pronunciation/grammar polishes.
- Assign the **60-second voice note**: *"Record a quick Friday Slack/WhatsApp voice recap confirming what is launching Monday vs. what is parked for Wave 2."*

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue & Extension
- **If she aces Run 1 easily:** Switch to **Executive Email Mode** for the last 5 minutes—have her type a 3-sentence stakeholder recap in Preply chat using `trade-off` and `aligned on priorities`.
- **If she gets stuck mid-pushback:** Pause the role-play, point to Block 2 on the canvas, ask *"Which of the 6 moves buys you breathing room here?"*, let her read the frame once, and resume.
