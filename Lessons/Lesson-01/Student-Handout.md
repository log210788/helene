## 1. The Tech Pronunciation Clinic: The "Stress-Timing" Reset

> In French, every syllable has roughly equal length, and the final syllable gets the emphasis.  
> In English, **one syllable is long and loud**, and the rest are shortened and relaxed into the **Schwa (/ə/)**.

### A. The 8 High-Frequency Tech Words

Tap your finger on the desk on the **CAPITALIZED** syllable:

| Word | English Rhythm & Stress | Phonetic Guide | Natural Work Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Development** | de-**VEL**-op-ment | `/dɪˈvel.əp.mənt/` | *"I oversee the backend **de-VEL-op-ment** of our ERP system."* |
| **Automotive** | **AU**-to-mo-tive | `/ˈɔː.tə.məʊ.tɪv/` | *"Our clients are leaders in the **AU-to-mo-tive** parts market."* |
| **Component** | com-**PO**-nent | `/kəmˈpəʊ.nənt/` | *"Each spare part is tracked as an individual **com-PO-nent**."* |
| **Architecture** | **AR**-chi-tec-ture | `/ˈɑː.kɪ.tek.tʃə/` | *"We’re optimizing the database **AR-chi-tec-ture** for speed."* |
| **Comfortable** | **COMF**-tə-bl *(3 syllables!)* | `/ˈkʌmf.tə.bəl/` | *"I want to feel **COMF-ta-ble** presenting in sprint meetings."* |

### B. The "H" Filter: Breathing vs. Silent

- **The Warm Air Rule**: Put your hand in front of your lips. When saying `/h/`, feel a gentle puff of warm air!
  - ✅ **H**ave, **H**ybrid, **H**osted, **H**ardware, **H**elp, **H**igh-priority.
- **Watch Out For The Vowel Traps**:
  - Don't add an "H" to words starting with a vowel:
    - *"An **ERP** system"* (not *"a herp system"*)
    - *"An **application**"* (not *"a happlication"*)
    - *"Our **operating** system"*

---

### C. The 3 Golden Rules for Past-Tense `-ed`

Never add an extra syllable unless the root verb ends in a **T** or a **D**!

| Group | When to use | Sound | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group 1** | Verbs ending in **T** or **D** | **+1 Extra Syllable** (`/ɪd/`) | *test-ed*, *up-dat-ed*, *con-nec-ted*, *in-sert-ed* |
| **Group 2** | Verbs ending in unvoiced sounds (`p`, `k`, `s`, `ch`, `sh`, `x`) | **Crisp /t/ sound** *(No extra syllable)* | *checked* (`/tʃekt/`), *fixed* (`/fɪkst/`), *developed* (`/dɪˈvel.əpt/`) |
| **Group 3** | All other verbs (voiced vowels, `m`, `n`, `l`, `r`, `v`, `g`) | **Smooth /d/ sound** *(No extra syllable)* | *configured* (`/kənˈfɪɡ.jəd/`), *deployed* (`/dɪˈplɔɪd/`), *solved* (`/sɒlvd/`) |

## 2. Breaking the Word-Search Freeze: The "Anti-Freeze" Toolkit

> **Rule of Thumb**: When you can't find a word in English, **never fall silent** and **never stop to translate from French**.  
> Use a **Discourse Anchor** to buy 2 seconds of thinking time!

### A. Professional Time-Buyers (Use these instead of *"Euhhh..."*)

1. **Buying Time Naturally**:
   - *"Essentially, what happens is..."*
   - *"To put it another way..."*
   - *"How can I put it... basically, ..."*
   - *"What I mean to say is..."*
2. **Structuring Complex Thoughts**:
   - *"From a technical standpoint, ..."*
   - *"Under the hood, the script is..."*
   - *"On the client's end, they notice that..."*
   - *"In a nutshell, we're looking at..."*

---

### B. The 3-Way Circumlocution Formula

If you forget the exact word for a technical tool, bug, or business concept, use one of these three angles:

```
                  ┌───────────────► 1. Function: "It's the feature/script that lets users..."
                  │
When you forget   ├───────────────► 2. Analogy:  "It's basically a bridge between..."
the exact word... │
                  └───────────────► 3. Impact:   "The main outcome is that it speeds up..."
```

## 4. Interactive Simulation: The 90-Second Sprint Standup

### Your Roleplay Scenario:
> You are in a daily standup meeting with your international colleagues or a client manager.  
> Give a brief 90-second update on a recent technical task or bug fix in your automotive ERP software.

#### Your 3-Step Speaking Checklist:
1. **Start with a Time-Buyer**: *"Essentially, over the past couple of days, I've been focused on..."*
2. **Describe the Challenge using Tech Rhythm**: Remember **com-PO-nent**, **de-VEL-op-ment**, and **AU-to-mo-tive**.
3. **State the Status using past-tense `-ed`**: *"We **tested** (/ɪd/) the fix, **deployed** (/d/) the patch, and **checked** (/t/) the logs."*
