Part 01 • Tutor’s Diagnostic

Spoken Fluency Analysis & Key Takeaways

A breakdown of your communicative strengths from Lesson 01 and the high-impact areas we are polishing next.

1. Great Conversational Drive

Just like in daily meetings when no one else takes the lead, you naturally push the momentum forward. When searching for a word in English (such as arroser, écharpe de portage, or éducation), you never froze—you described the concept clearly until we locked in the exact expression.

2. Clear, Expressive Ideas

You express nuanced opinions and comparisons with ease—whether discussing generational shifts in upbringing, workplace leadership, seasonal weather patterns, or why solo swing dancing is "just like riding a bike."

3. Our Main Growth Focus

Our top priority is eliminating direct French-to-English translations of everyday verbs, nouns, and prepositions: upgrading "learn to someone" to "teach someone", "education" to "upbringing", and "hard with" to "hard on".
